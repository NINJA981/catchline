About

Paste a promo page. See the catch next to every claim.

The same scanner, matcher and catch extractor as the browser extension. It finds every starred claim, pulls it up to its fine print, and lists the fees and conditions.

Runs entirely in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

Browsers don’t let a web page fetch other sites’ pages (CORS), so Catchline can’t open a link for you. Open the offer, select all, copy, and paste it here. Pasting page source (View source) works best; plain text works too.
